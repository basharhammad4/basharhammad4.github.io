2Disciplines
GRC consulting on an offensive security foundation

I run end-to-end cybersecurity risk assessments for banks against NIST CSF 2.0, ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and the SAMA, CBJ and CBI frameworks, rating likelihood and impact per control and backing every rating with reviewed evidence.

I assess current state against required state control by control, then prioritise the gap list by regulatory exposure and remediation effort so the client closes what matters before the deadline that matters.

I map control evidence across ISO 27001, CIS v8, PCI DSS v4.0 and the central-bank requirements, so one body of evidence satisfies several frameworks and audits stop asking for the same artefact three times.

I build and maintain risk registers with calculated risk and impact levels, owners and treatment dates, and I measure and report cybersecurity KPIs in a form leadership can track quarter on quarter.

I lead stakeholder meetings and consultations with business and technical owners, walking them through findings and agreeing treatment plans that are realistic for the people who deliver them.

I deliver security awareness sessions for client organisations and write policies to the framework clause they satisfy, in Arabic or English.
